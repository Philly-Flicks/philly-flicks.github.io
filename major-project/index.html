UNDERSTANDING RACISM IN AMERICA

LEARN THE HISTORY · RECOGNIZE THE PRESENT · EXPLORE WAYS TO CREATE CHANGE

WHAT IS RACISM?

Racism is more than individual prejudice, it is a system of beliefs, actions, and structures that create unequal treatment and opportunity based on race. It can appear through personal interactions, institutions, media, education, housing, and policies that affect everyday life.

WHY IT MATTERS

Understanding racism helps reveal how history, systems, and modern issues continue to shape people’s experiences today. By learning about inequality and its impact, we become better equipped to recognize injustice, challenge harmful systems, and create more informed conversations about change.
