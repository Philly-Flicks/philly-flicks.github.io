ABOUT ME

I’m Philip Hazeley, a multimedia creator focused on photography, graphic design, video editing, and visual storytelling. My work is built around capturing people, moments, and stories with intention. Outside of my professional work, I enjoy anything that fuels creativity. Whether it's watching movies (shoutout to AMC A-List), playing video games, or listening to music, I’m always looking for new sources of inspiration. These interests help keep my creativity fresh and often influence the way I approach storytelling, design, and content creation.
