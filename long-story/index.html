Beyond the Walls

A long-scrolling story inspired by Attack on Titan.

1. A World Surrounded

For generations, humanity lived behind towering walls, believing they were the last people alive. The walls gave them safety, but they also kept them trapped.

2. The Day Everything Changed

One sudden attack shattered the illusion of peace. The wall was broken, fear poured in, and a young soldier’s dream of freedom turned into a promise of revenge.

3. Training for Survival

Survivors became soldiers. They learned to fly through cities, fight impossible enemies, and trust the people beside them even when the world gave them no reason to hope.

4. The Truth Beneath the Story

The enemy was not as simple as it seemed. Beyond the walls were secrets, history, and people who had been shaped by the same cycle of fear and hatred.

5. Freedom Comes With a Cost

In the end, the fight was never only about defeating monsters. It was about choosing what kind of world should exist after the violence finally stopped.

The End

The walls fall. The story ends. But the question remains: what does freedom mean when it is built on sacrifice?
